What Is AI?

AI is a smart assistant that can read, write, suggest, and sometimes act — but it does not think like a human.

Think of it like this

A very fast intern who can draft text, find patterns, and follow instructions.

It is not perfect

AI can make mistakes. Always review before you post.

You type a request. AI gives you a response.

Examples

  • ChatGPT
  • Comet
  • Built-in AI in apps

Good for

  • Captions & hooks
  • Hashtag ideas
  • Re-writing text

Level 2 AI: Agentic AI

AI that remembers context and completes steps for you.

Example

OpenClaw: an AI assistant that can schedule, search, and create across tools.

Good for

  • Planning a content calendar
  • Reminders and follow-ups
  • Everyday productivity

We will demo this later with Dola, an AI calendar assistant.

11
Part 1

What AI Can and Cannot Do

✅ AI can

  • Suggest captions and hashtags
  • Summarise long text
  • Help brainstorm content ideas
  • Rephrase your words

❌ AI cannot

  • Know your real friends or feelings
  • Guarantee facts are true
  • Replace your own voice
  • Decide what is right to post

Basic Prompting for Social Media

A good prompt tells AI what you want, who it is for, and how it should sound.

“Give me 3 short captions for Instagram about my weekend baking. Friendly tone. Under 30 words. Add 3 hashtags.”

Try it now on your phone if you are ready.

Shooting Tips

Light

Face a window or light source.

Sound

Minimise background noise. Captions help everyone.

Steady

Hold with both hands or rest your elbow.

If you do not want to be on camera, you can create text-only or photo-based content.

Staying Safe & Smart Online

Do not share

  • NRIC number
  • Bank details
  • Passwords
  • Home address

Always check

  • Facts before posting
  • Other people’s consent
  • AI-generated faces or voices
  • Your caption before publishing
