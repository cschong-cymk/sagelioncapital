Access workshops.

Hands-on sessions for the deaf community, people with disabilities, and young seniors. You touch the tools, keep the files, and leave with something that actually works.

Who they're for

  • Deaf participants — sessions with sign interpretation, captions, and visual-first instruction.
  • People with disabilities — adaptive setups, patience, and practical tools matched to ability.
  • Young seniors — no assumptions, no rushing, plenty of time to try, ask, and try again.
