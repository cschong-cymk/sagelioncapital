Why it matters

"Their eyes were open."

That's what a participant said after our June workshop: a PMET in his 40s who came in worried and left with files, prompts, and a small portfolio he could point to. One seat, one Saturday morning, one person no longer left out of the conversation.

Come for the paintings. Stay to find out what else we do.

Our exhibitions are how we introduce ourselves: seventeen works from 2017 to 2026, in oil, acrylic, pencil, and mixed media. The art is the front door, and the talks and workshops are in the next room.
