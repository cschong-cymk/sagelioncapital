Mission

Restructuring doesn't send a memo first.

We want people to see it coming and have somewhere to go. Sagelion is a donor-funded studio in Singapore that teaches AI, free, to the people the tech rollout leaves behind, and runs free talks on looking after your body, your mind and your home.

Sometimes restructuring makes you feel like the only dinosaur in the room. We get it. That's exactly who we're here for.

What we believe
Built for real peopleMostly PMETs feeling the restructuring pressure. We're just as glad to have the deaf community, people with disabilities, and young seniors in the room. Everyone sits at the same table. 01
Rigour, not reassuranceRun by people who've sat through real restructurings. We teach what actually holds up: practical skills, honest limits, and no pep talks about "the future of work." 02
Free for the people we serveDonors fund every community workshop and talk seat, so no learner pays. Two things do cost money, and we label them clearly: OPERATOR, our professional certification, and the children's camp. We steward every dollar so it reaches the people in the room. 03
Why this exists

PMETs are getting the sharp end of this.

Roles reshaped or cut, often with little warning. Our founders spent 25 to 37 years in finance and tech, watching companies restructure from the inside. Sagelion is them putting that experience to use for the people it's actually happening to.

We don't think everyone needs to become an engineer. We think they need to be the person in the room who isn't afraid of the tools: someone who can read the risk early, build a small working toolkit, and leave with proof, not promises.
