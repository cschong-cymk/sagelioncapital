For working professionals

OPERATOR: The Enterprise AI Certification

A 3-day programme for operations leads, business analysts, tech-savvy founders, and team leads. Roughly 30% concepts and strategy, 70% hands-on labs, finishing with a live practical capstone and a real certification.

Length3 days
FeePaid programme (not donor-funded)
Day 1

Architecture, enterprise integration and advanced tooling

Move from prompting to operating: context management, deterministic vs probabilistic execution, and how an agentic framework is put together. Cover enterprise security basics (prompt-injection defence, data classification, tool-access threat modelling), then extend agents with custom tools, MCP servers, and live API integrations. Lab: build an enterprise-ready operator setup.

Day 2

Multi-agent orchestration, stakeholder management and guardrails

Design single-agent, hierarchical, and peer-to-peer networks, and connect them to service-desk and incident-management workflows. Practise emotional intelligence and working with internal stakeholders, then lock in memory management, context optimisation, human-in-the-loop triggers, and disaster recovery. Lab: build and test an automated ticket-triage workflow end to end.

Day 3

Transformation, analytics and certification capstone

Map processes and build ROI analytics tied to IT department KPIs. Learn maintenance, version control, and SOP hand-off. Then sit a live 3-hour capstone: architect a secure workspace, integrate a mock ticketing system, and produce a verification, compliance, and ROI framework. Pass and graduate certified.
