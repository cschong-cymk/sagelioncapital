Field notes 30 September 2026 · AI Workshop for PMETs

Third AI workshop for PMETs: hands-on, community-powered, and free.

An intensive 3.5-hour session where participants installed OpenClaw, past attendees returned as facilitators, and one participant saw exactly how bots can handle repetitive tasks.

We recently wrapped up our third AI Workshop, a 3.5-hour intensive session designed specifically for PMETs — Professionals, Managers, Executives, and Technicians. With about 15 participants in the room, the energy was high, and the outcomes were even better than we hoped.

Learning by doing

The highlight of the session was the hands-on component. Participants didn't just listen to concepts; they rolled up their sleeves and installed the OpenClaw runtime directly on their machines. Watching attendees go from first-time exposure to actively experimenting with an AI agent runtime in a single session is exactly why we run these workshops.

The positive feedback on the hands-on portion tells us we're hitting the right note: practical, accessible, and immediately useful.

A community effort

This workshop was made stronger by volunteers from previous workshops who returned as facilitators. Their guidance kept the room moving, answered questions in real time, and helped newer participants build confidence. Seeing past attendees become today's helpers is one of the most rewarding signs that the program is building real momentum.

Participant spotlight: Chandhan from SPD

A special shout-out to Chandhan, who joined us from SPD (Society for the Physically Disabled). His takeaway captured exactly why accessible AI training matters:

“Learning new bots can assist with repetitive tasks.”

Moments like this remind us that AI upskilling isn't just about technology — it's about empowering people to work smarter and more independently.

Supported by the SG Partnerships Fund

Thanks to generous funding from the SG Partnerships Fund, this workshop was completely free for participants. We believe cost should never be a barrier to AI literacy, and this support lets us keep the door open to everyone who wants to learn.

More than just learning

Of course, no good workshop is complete without coffee and snacks. Participants also enjoyed the refreshments provided — a small but important part of creating a welcoming, relaxed environment where people can focus and connect.

What's next

We remain committed to delivering accessible and impactful AI training for Singapore's workforce. Whether you're a PMET looking to upskill, a past participant interested in volunteering, or an organisation seeking partnership opportunities, we'd love to hear from you.

Stay tuned for our next session — and thank you to everyone who joined, helped, and supported this workshop.
